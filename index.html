Privacy Policy

Sticker Pop · Little Stage · Pocket Pal · last updated October 30, 2026

1. Summary

Sticker Pop, Little Stage and Pocket Pal (each “the app”) are talking pet apps published by Cool App Helpdesk. You look after an animal character, dress it up, and speak to it so that it repeats what you said in a funny voice. This policy covers all three apps, which handle data in the same way.

  • There is no account or sign-in, and we (the developer) never receive any data about you or your recordings.
  • The microphone is used only while you hold the talk button. Your voice is kept in memory so the character can repeat it, and is never uploaded.
  • The apps do not have the Android internet permission, so they cannot send anything over the internet.
  • The apps contain no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

2. Data we collect

The developer collects nothing. We have no server and receive no personal data, voice recordings, usage data or analytics from the apps.

3. Data stored on your device

Everything the app keeps is stored in its private storage on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. The app opts out of Android cloud backup, so this data is not copied to your Google account either.

5. Network activity and third parties

The apps make no network connections and contain no third-party SDK that collects data. All voice effects are applied on your device.

When you share a voice clip through the system share sheet, the app hands the clip to the app you choose; the developer does not see what you share. Privacy policy and Contact us in the More screen open your browser or email app, which then handle the link under their own policies.

6. Children’s privacy

The apps are intended for general audiences and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the developer collects no personal information from anyone. Voice recordings are never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

7. Data retention and deletion

The developer stores no data off your device. Your latest voice take disappears when the app closes. To remove everything else, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → the app → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

8. Security

App data is held in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s built-in application sandboxing, and is excluded from Android cloud backup. The apps are distributed through the Google Play Store, which verifies the developer signing key on every update.

9. Changes to this policy

If any material aspect of how the apps handle data ever changes, this page will be updated and the “last updated” date above will be bumped. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
